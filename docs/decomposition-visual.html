01 · General mechanism

A guarded reverse pipeline

The module is not a generic retrosynthesis engine. It recognizes one requested linkage family, repairs the precise precursor endpoints, then refuses to serialize unless the recovered role set is complete.

The chemistry and the periodic graph travel together. Molecular fragments recover precursor identity; periodic edge gains recover the net.

02 · Linkage atlas

Eight strategies, two shapes

Six binary bridges cut bonds between precursor instances. Two ring-forming strategies open a strict product ring and reconstruct one precursor class.

The reaction lines are recognition schematics, not balanced synthetic equations. “Repair” states what the implementation adds or rewrites after fragmentation.

03 · Conflict resolution

Specificity before cutting

Conflict resolution is conservative at two scales: per-bond classification for overlapping C–N/C=N motifs, then complete periodic-hypothesis checks where one family can masquerade as another.

Local ambiguity withholds only the implicated bond; a complete competing periodic decomposition withholds the triazine assignment.

Nitrogen linkage resolver

Every eligible inter-instance C–N/C=N is evaluated independently.

N–N environment branch

  1. C=N–N=C?
    Assign azine to the paired eligible bonds.
  2. C=N–N–C(=O)?

  3. Neither?

Keto-enamine branch

  1. Ring-adjacent carbonyl?
    Assign β-ketoenamine.
  2. No keto environment?
    Fall back to generic imine.

04 · Topology recovery

Fragments become a periodic gain graph

Cut chemistry defines the nodes and incidences. CIF image offsets become integer edge gains; their independent cycle rank supplies the dimensionality hint used for conservative topology matching.

Ring-forming products need an extra abstraction: each recovered B₃O₃ or C₃N₃ event becomes a virtual 3-connected topology node.

1. Record periodic cuts

Each cut knows both fragment membership and the lattice image connecting its atoms.

(fragmentᵢ, fragmentⱼ, gain)

2. Abstract ring events

Three ring anchors converge on a virtual node. Ditopic precursor fragments collapse into edges; higher-connected fragments remain nodes.

virtual connectivity = 3

3. Match invariantly

Normalize equivalent stacked components, calculate gain-cycle rank, then compare available topology graphs under switching and integer basis changes.

cycle-gain rank → 1D / 2D / 3D
embedded COFidAccepted first when a valid structure comment supplies topology.
named-stack collapseEquivalent disconnected layers are reduced to one representative graph.
vertex switchingGain labels may shift with node-image choices without changing the net.
GL(ℤ) basis trialsCandidate unimodular lattice transforms remove basis-choice artifacts.

05 · Result contract

Only one path emits a COFid

A plausible cut is intermediate evidence. Success additionally requires complete roles, a resolved topology, and a COFid that parses into a valid build request.

✓

success

Every expected precursor role was recovered exactly once as a unique monomer block; topology resolved; serialized COFid passed parse and build validation.

–

skipped

Unsupported linkage, no eligible chemistry, locally ambiguous requested bonds, incomplete role recovery, precedence suppression, or unresolved topology.

!

error

Malformed CIF, unsupported non-P1 symmetry, self-image quotient bonding, invalid bond mode, unknown topology, or another structural exception.

i

Scope boundary: the module targets COFKit’s eight buildable linkage strategies and conservative topology repository. It does not expand arbitrary asymmetric units or infer unrestricted third-party reaction history.
